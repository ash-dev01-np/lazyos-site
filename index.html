LazyOS

Your whole life, one app. Quietly doing the boring parts for you.

LazyOS is a personal command centre for Android. It pulls your email, documents, deadlines, money, job hunt and big life admin into one place, then tells you what actually needs attention. It runs on a private server you own, so your data stays yours.

Inbox radarImportant email surfaces fast. Everything else stays quiet.
Find anythingOne search across mail and documents.
Deadlines & milestonesDates found for you, not buried in a thread.
Money clarityWhat you have, what's coming, what's safe to spend.
Opportunity finderJobs and deals matched to you.
Private by designEncrypted, read-only by default, no ads, no selling data.

Today LazyOS is a private build for its creator. A version you can personalise for yourself is planned.
