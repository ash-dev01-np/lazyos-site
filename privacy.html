LazyOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

What LazyOS accesses

With the user's permission, LazyOS can read their Gmail (the read-only gmail.readonly permission). It cannot send, delete, move, label or change any email.

How the data is used and stored

Email data is copied to a private server controlled by the user, on an encrypted disk. It is used only to show the user their important emails, let them search it, and send them a notification. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models.

LazyOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Users can disconnect LazyOS from their Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which stops all access. Stored copies can be deleted from the user's own server.
